Why Triangle Flooring

What Makes Our Installations Different

Most flooring companies sell you product. We engineer a floor that survives Florida's humidity, hurricane season, and decades of foot traffic.

01

The Triangle 42-Point Standard

Our proprietary checklist covers subfloor moisture testing, expansion gaps, transition strips, and 38 other steps every install must pass before signoff.

02

Florida-Acclimated Materials

Every plank, tile, and board acclimates 48–72 hours on-site at your home's actual humidity — preventing the buckling and gapping that plagues coastal installs.

03

Same Crew Start-to-Finish

The installer who measures your home is the same one who finishes the last baseboard. No subcontractor handoffs, no quality drop-off mid-project.

04

1-Year Labor Warranty

Written, no-fine-print warranty on every installation. If a plank lifts, a tile cracks at the grout, or a tread squeaks — we fix it. No questions.

05

Free 24-Hour Estimates

Call before noon, get an in-home measurement and written quote within 24 hours. No deposits, no high-pressure sales, no obligation.

06

Hurricane Damage Specialists

We've completed 80+ insurance and post-storm reflooring projects from Hurricane Ian and Helene. Documentation-ready, claim-friendly workflow.
